Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about shopping, shipping, and getting the most from GrowPal.

Did not find what you need? Contact us or chat with our expert flow for situational advice.

What is GrowPal?

GrowPal is a smart green marketplace: we help you pick plants and supplies that fit your climate zone, space, and goals—whether you have a balcony, a courtyard, or a full garden.

How do climate zones work here?

Our map and city profiles combine typical rainfall, summer heat, and elevation cues to suggest plants and care levels that are realistic for your area. It is guidance, not a guarantee against extreme weather—always observe your own micro-climate.

Do you ship plants everywhere?

Availability depends on season, stock, and courier coverage. During checkout you will see what applies to your cart. Fragile items may ship in waves—we pack to reduce transit stress.

What if my plant arrives damaged?

Photograph the plant and packaging within 48 hours and contact us through the contact page. We will walk you through a replacement or store credit when it is clearly a transit or packing issue.

Can I change or cancel an order?

If the item has not shipped yet, we can usually adjust or cancel. Once it is with the courier, routing changes may be limited—reach out as soon as possible.
